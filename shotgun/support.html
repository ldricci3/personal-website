Support

We're here to help.

Shotgun AI is currently in beta. If you're experiencing issues or have feedback, we'd love to hear from you.

Contact Us

Email us at support@leo-ricci.com and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

Bug Reports

The fastest way to report a bug is to use the in-app shake gesture. This automatically captures a screenshot, device info, and recent logs so we can diagnose the issue quickly.

You can also email us directly with a description of the issue, the steps to reproduce it, and your device model and iOS version.

Feature Requests

Have an idea for Shotgun AI? We're actively developing new features and value user feedback. Send your suggestions to support@leo-ricci.com.

FAQ

What data does Shotgun AI collect?

Shotgun AI uses your location to provide navigation. We also collect anonymous crash reports and usage analytics to improve the app. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Which devices are supported?

Shotgun AI requires an iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later.

Is Shotgun AI free?

Shotgun AI is free during the beta period.
