Shotgun AI is a voice-first navigation app for iOS. Talk to find places, get directions, and navigate — all without touching the screen.

🎙️

Voice-First

Search for places, ask questions, and control navigation entirely by voice. Designed for drivers who need their hands on the wheel and eyes on the road.

🧠

AI Copilot

More than a nav app — Shotgun understands context. Ask it to find the nearest gas station, search for a category of places, or get help deciding where to go.

🗺️

Turn-by-Turn Navigation

Full turn-by-turn directions powered by Google Maps. Lane guidance, traffic awareness, and real-time rerouting built in.
