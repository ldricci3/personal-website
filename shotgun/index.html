Features

Navigation, reimagined for your voice.

Shotgun AI is built for drivers. Every interaction is designed to work without looking at or touching your phone.

Voice-First Interface

Tap to talk or use full-screen tap-to-talk. Search for places, ask for recommendations, start navigation — all hands-free. Designed for when your eyes belong on the road.

AI That Understands Context

Not just a voice command layer. Shotgun's AI understands what you need — "find a gas station on my route," "somewhere good for lunch nearby," or "what's closest."

Turn-by-Turn Navigation

Full turn-by-turn directions powered by Google Maps. Lane guidance, real-time traffic, and automatic rerouting. The navigation you trust, with an AI copilot on top.

Along-Route Search

Need to stop for coffee or gas? Shotgun searches along your active route, not just nearby. Results show how far off your path each option is — no guessing.

Natural Conversation

Speak naturally, not in commands. "Take me to the closest Whole Foods" works. So does "actually, let's go to Trader Joe's instead." Shotgun adapts to how you talk.

Smart Place Results

When you search, Shotgun reads out what it found so you can pick without looking. Tap to preview, or just say "the first one." Route preview shows you the way before you commit.

How It Works

Three steps. No screens required.

1

Talk

Tap the mic or use full-screen tap-to-talk. Tell Shotgun where you want to go, what you're looking for, or ask a question about your trip.

2

Listen

Shotgun reads back search results, distances, and options so you can decide without looking at the screen. It speaks, you choose.

3

Drive

Say "start navigation" or tap go. Turn-by-turn voice guidance takes over. Need something else mid-drive? Just talk again.
